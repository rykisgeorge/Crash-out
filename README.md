# 🎮 Crash Game

A thrilling web-based game where you test your timing and risk management with fake points. Watch the multiplier climb and decide when to cash out before it crashes!

## 🚀 Play Now

**[Play the Game →](https://rykisgeorge.github.io/Crash-out/)**

## 📖 How to Play

1. **Start with 1000 points** - You begin each session with fake points
2. **Place your bet** - Choose how many points to risk
3. **Watch the multiplier** - The multiplier increases chaotically from 1.00x upward
4. **Cash out in time** - Click "Cash Out" to lock in your winnings before the crash
5. **Winnings calculation** - `Final Points = Bet × Multiplier`

### ⚡ Game Mechanics

The crash point is randomly determined with weighted probabilities:

- **55% probability** - Low crashes (1.00x - 2.70x)
- **28% probability** - Medium crashes (2.70x - 7.70x)  
- **13% probability** - High crashes (7.00x - 22.00x)
- **4% probability** - Rare massive runs (20x - 120x)

## 🎯 Strategy Tips

- ⚠️ Most games crash within the first multiplier range - be cautious with greedy betting
- 💰 Occasional massive spikes happen, but they're rare - don't chase them every time
- 🎲 The house edge is built into the probability distribution
- 🧠 Consistent small wins beat risky all-or-nothing plays
- 🎉 Remember: these are fake points, just have fun!
